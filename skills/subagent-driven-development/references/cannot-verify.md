# Resolve Missing Review Evidence

A reviewer may be unable to verify a requirement from the provided snapshot. Identify the exact requirement and needed source, execution result, or cross-task interface. Read that context or obtain the appropriate check; do not treat missing evidence as proof either of correctness or of a defect.

Resolve material acceptance gaps before completion. If verification is unavailable, state the limitation and its impact; an important unresolved correctness concern remains incomplete. Route real findings through [fix-loop](fix-loop.md) with their actual severity.
